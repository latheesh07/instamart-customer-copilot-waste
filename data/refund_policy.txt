DEMO REFUND POLICY

This is a fictional policy created for the Instamart Customer Intelligence & Support Copilot capstone project. It is not an official Instamart policy.

1. Damaged Product:
Customers reporting damaged products should provide their order ID and a description of the issue. The request must be reviewed before a decision is made.

2. Missing Item:
Customers reporting missing items should provide their order ID and identify the missing product. The request must be reviewed.

3. Wrong Item:
Customers who receive an incorrect item should provide their order ID and describe the item received.

4. Refund Requests:
Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. All requests remain Pending Review until checked by an authorized reviewer.

5. Privacy:
Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, OTPs, or other sensitive information in support messages.

6. Final Decision:
This demo assistant cannot approve refunds or issue payments. Refund decisions require human review.

IMPORTANT:
This document is synthetic demonstration content, not a real company policy.
